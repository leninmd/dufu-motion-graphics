# 10 · 离屏粒子草堂 · 子弹时间 / Offscreen Particle Dissolve & Bullet Time · 761 · 3,100 km
Implemented in `src/scenes/scene_10.js` as the particle benchmark.

An 8,000-particle glyph roof is ripped off by the wind. At u=2.0 the time
integrator γ(t) drops to 0.012, rain becomes suspended glass beads, and the roof
shards assemble into the "广厦千万间" couplet. In bar 3 time resumes and the
particles rise into a luminous canopy.
