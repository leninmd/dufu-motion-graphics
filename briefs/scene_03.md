# 03 · 三维水墨点云 · 望岳 / 3D Point Cloud Perspective · 736 · 705 km
Implemented in `src/scenes/scene_03.js` as the first benchmark.

The range is 35,000 seeded particles, volume-filled enough to read as mountain
mass. Bar 1 shows the naive panorama and the young traveller. At u=2.0 the camera
rotates 90° in orthographic space and the x dimension collapses into a single
silhouette, while a large brush couplet appears in the right negative space.
